SaaS Development FAQs

Key questions on building, launching, and budgeting a SaaS platform.

A lean SaaS MVP focused on one core workflow and authentication typically ranges from $12,000 to $18,000. Full-featured SaaS platforms with recurring Stripe billing, multi-role workspaces, and interactive analytics dashboards generally range from $18,000 to $35,000+.
Most MVPs launch within 6 to 10 weeks. We focus heavily on scoping the core value proposition first so you can begin acquiring beta users and generating revenue as early as possible.
We implement row-level security (RLS) and schema-level isolation policies in PostgreSQL/Supabase to guarantee that tenant data is never exposed across organization boundaries.
